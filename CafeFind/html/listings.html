The Roasted Bean

★★★★★ 4.9 · 218 reviews ££
Save to Favorites Get Directions
Free Wi-Fi Power Outlets Wheelchair Accessible Quiet Study Zone Independent

Tucked into a Victorian railway arch in Manchester's Northern Quarter, The Roasted Bean is the city's go-to spot for serious coffee and focused work. Sourcing beans directly from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, their espresso bar is run by competition-trained baristas. The hushed atmosphere, no-calls policy, and reliable 200 Mbps Wi-Fi make it a firm favourite among students and remote workers.

Address 14 Cotton Street, Northern Quarter, Manchester M4 5BF Hours Mon–Fri 7am–7pm · Sat–Sun 8am–6pm Wi-Fi Free · Password on receipt Seating 38 seats · Quiet zone available Phone 0161 234 5678 Website www.roastedbean.example.com

100% Vegan Outdoor Seating Pet Friendly Free Wi-Fi Plant-based Café

Green Grounds occupies a converted Victorian terrace with a hidden walled garden in Chorlton. Every item on the menu is 100% plant-based, sourced within 50 miles where possible. Their oat-milk lattes have won awards three years running, and the weekend brunch queue regularly stretches down the street. Dogs welcome; cats have been spotted.

Student Tips

  • 1 Always check Wi-Fi speed reviews before planning a study session — some free Wi-Fi cafés enforce heavy speed caps.
  • 2 Power outlet availability varies. Arrive early for a seat near a socket, or check the café profile first.
  • 3 Buy at least one drink per 90–120 minutes to stay on good terms with staff — it keeps seats available for everyone.
  • 4 Noise-cancelling headphones are invaluable in busier cafés. Reserve quiet-zone seats for reading or revision.
  • 5 Midweek mornings (9–11am) are the quietest times at most CaféFind listings.
  • 6 Some cafés offer student discounts — look for the Student Discount badge on listing cards.
