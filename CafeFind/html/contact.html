FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

For Visitors

Is CaféFind free to use?

Yes — browsing, searching, and creating a customer account are completely free, now and always.

How do I save a café to my list?

Create a free account and use the Save button on any café listing or search result to add it to your profile.

How do reviews work?

Registered users can leave a star rating and written review for any café. We moderate reviews to remove spam but do not edit genuine opinions.

Can I report inaccurate café information?

Yes — use the Suggest an Edit button on any café profile, or contact us via the form above and we will update it promptly.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account, then Settings, then Delete Account. Alternatively email us at support@cafefind.example.com and we will handle it within 48 hours.

For Café Owners

How do I get my café listed?

Fill in the submission form on the For Owners page. Basic listings are free and go live within 24 hours after review.

Can I update my café information after listing?

Yes — log in to your owner account and edit your profile at any time. Changes go live immediately after a quick automated check.

What is the difference between Basic and Pro plans?

Basic is free and includes a standard listing. Pro (£9/month) adds featured placement, an analytics dashboard, and a photo gallery. See the full comparison on the For Owners page.

How do I respond to a review?

Owner accounts can reply directly to any review from the Owner Dashboard. Responses appear publicly below the original review.

Can I remove a negative review?

We do not remove genuine reviews. If a review violates our guidelines — spam, hate speech, or false claims — contact us and we will investigate.
